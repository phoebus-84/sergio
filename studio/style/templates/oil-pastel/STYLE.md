# {{unit.title}} — oil pastel and gouache

**What happens.** {{unit.idea}}

## The look
It must look like a scan of oil pastel and gouache on {{palette.paper.name}}
({{palette.paper}}), drawn in a naive, bold, geometric mid-century picture-book
manner. Colours: {{palette}} — and nothing else.

## How it is built
Draw everything as flat 2D shapes, then build a real stroke engine: every shape
is filled with visible directional pastel strokes that skip over the paper
tooth and let the paper show through; edges are rough and slightly overshoot;
colours layer where they overlap. Sgraffito highlights: {{strokes.sgraffito}}.
Flat matte gouache areas with faint brush drag sit beside the waxy pastel.
Heavy paper grain, lit from {{strokes.light}}.

## How it moves
Re-draw the strokes every {{strokes.boil}} so the whole picture boils gently;
characters move on twos; smears behind moving things: {{strokes.smears}}.
Stage foreground, midground and background on {{staging.layers}} multiplane
layers with one designed move: {{staging.camera}}. An opening image, something
that happens, and a final image that lands.
