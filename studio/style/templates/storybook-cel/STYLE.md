# {{unit.title}} — storybook cel animation

**What happens.** {{unit.idea}}

## The look
Classic hand-drawn cel animation in the tradition of mid-century feature
animation, with original characters and world only. Backgrounds are painted
procedurally as {{camera.layers}} flat watercolour and gouache layers — wet
edges, pigment granulation, soft bleeds, dry-brush foliage — in {{palette}}.

## How it is built
Characters are drawn each frame as inked and painted cels from deformable 2D
splines: flat opaque fills inside a {{cel.taper}} ink line in {{cel.line}};
self-colour lines: {{cel.self-line}}; a soft cel shadow on the background.
Hand-drawn effects cels for ripples, sparkles in {{palette.glow.name}}, dust
puffs. One shader photographs it all: paper tooth, cel flicker and weave, dust,
grain, gate weave, vignette, {{camera.finish}} print colour.

## How it moves
From a keyed pose sheet, on {{cel.fast}} for fast action and {{cel.rest}}
elsewhere: anticipation, squash and stretch, overlap and follow-through, arcs,
slow in and out, strong silhouettes. A {{camera.move}} on the multiplane
camera. An opening image, an event, a final image that lands.
