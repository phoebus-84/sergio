# {{unit.title}} — stop-motion cut paper

**What happens.** {{unit.idea}}

## The look
Stop-motion cut-paper collage, built entirely in code. Every element is a
piece of paper from a small procedural paper system: {{paper.stocks}}. The
papers' colours are {{palette}}. Cut: {{paper.cut}} (scissor-cut pieces have
slightly faceted edges, torn ones a white fibrous deckle), each with slight
curl and a soft contact shadow that grows as it lifts.

## How it is built
Layer the pieces on {{palette.board.name}} ({{palette.board}}); tape, pins and
fasteners visible: {{paper.fixings}}. Characters are jointed cut-out puppets.

## How it moves
On {{camera.timing}}: pieces slide, hinge and are swapped for replacement
shapes, with {{paper.jitter}} of jitter and shadows that flicker as if
re-photographed under a rostrum camera. {{camera.layers}} multiplane layers,
a designed pan and a push-in on the main character. Ending on the whole collage
flat on the board: {{camera.ending}}. Lamp falloff, slight lens softness, grain.
